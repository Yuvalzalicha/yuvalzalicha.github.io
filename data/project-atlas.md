## Public allowlist rule

A project or project abstraction appears here only when all of the following are true:

- The public purpose is clear.
- The wording does not expose client, partner, personal, commercial, legal, security, or credential information.
- The description does not reveal private architecture, source locations, internal methods, operating records, or confidential performance data.
- Academic work is separated from independent research and industrial experience.
- Business and client-adjacent work is generalized and does not imply publication authority for the underlying project.
- Private learning systems do not reveal course names, providers, syllabi, labs, licensed materials, or internal production methods.

## Intentionally excluded

- Original names of private business initiatives, products, client systems, and private learning programs
- Client or partner identities, contacts, contracts, pricing, targets, sales information, and operational data
- Credentials, private endpoints, repository paths, source locations, access configurations, and storage topology
- Department structures, decision ledgers, schedules, runbooks, internal IDs, and proprietary methods
- Restricted research details, security assumptions, unpublished results, and unsupported claims
- Course-owned materials, restricted exam solutions, private submissions, and unpublished academic files

Detailed academic case studies are retained only where the material is appropriate for public presentation. All other entries are generalized or restricted-detail summaries.
