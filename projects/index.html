Silicon Photonics Modeling & Simulation

Objective. Build device-level intuition and quantitative models for guided-wave silicon photonics using Tidy3D and Python.

Work performed. Rectangular and slot waveguide eigenmode analysis; effective-index and higher-order-mode studies; TE/TM-like field interpretation; directional-coupler supermode classification; coupling-length calculation; FDTD transfer simulation; ring- and Fabry-Pérot-resonator analysis.

Selected result. The selected 500 × 220 nm silicon directional-coupler configuration at 1.55 μm produced approximately 99.9% simulated transfer for the 100 nm gap at the modeled coupling length.

Tidy3DFDTDEigenmodesPythonResonators

Limitations: academic simulation only. No fabrication, PDK, GDS, tape-out, cleanroom, packaging, or industrial PIC experience is claimed.

Quantum-Circuit Error-Sensitivity Research

Objective. Examine how injected quantum errors affect different qubits and locations in a QPE-style circuit, and compare alternative notions of impact.

Work performed. Python/Qiskit simulations under X and Z injections; comparison of fidelity, corrected Hilbert-Schmidt loss, total-variation distance, purity and entropy, and task-level output metrics; validation checks and explicit model-limit documentation.

Interpretation. Different metrics can produce different sensitivity rankings, so state-level damage should not automatically be treated as task-level failure.

QiskitQPEFidelityTVDValidation

Limitations: the work does not yet demonstrate a decoder, logical-fidelity improvement, resource saving, or operational quantum error correction.
