Evidence and scope

Professional claims with visible boundaries.

This portfolio presents selected public work and distinguishes academic, exploratory, laboratory, and independent systems experience.

Evidence standards

Project descriptions are based on completed academic work, documented laboratory activity, ongoing exploratory research, or independent systems work. Status and limitations are stated where they materially affect interpretation.

Key claim boundaries

  • The B.Sc. is not described as completed while final degree requirements remain.
  • Silicon-photonics work is academic modeling and simulation, not fabrication, tape-out, PDK, or industrial PIC experience.
  • Course laboratory work is not presented as independent research ownership or industrial characterization.
  • Quantum-circuit sensitivity work is not presented as demonstrated quantum error correction.
  • Development-stage sensing work is not presented as validated performance or operational readiness.

Public project scope

This is a selected public portfolio, not a complete inventory. Private, client-owned, commercially sensitive, security-sensitive, licensed, and restricted academic material is not published.
