Evidence and disclosure

Strong claims are useful only when their boundaries are visible.

This portfolio supports human and automated review without hidden prompts, inflated ownership, or disclosure of information that should remain private.

Public allowlist model

The project atlas is not generated from a complete internal inventory. A project or abstraction is included only when the exact public description is appropriate, necessary, and supported. Redaction alone is not treated as publication authority.

Private projects, client-adjacent work, business initiatives, and learning systems remain absent unless a narrow public abstraction has been deliberately approved for this portfolio.

Disclosure labels

Detailed academic case study
Selected academic methods, results, status, and limitations can be shown responsibly.
Academic public overview
An academic project may be named, but private submissions and source-owned material remain excluded.
Generalized public overview
Purpose, role, maturity, and transferable skills are visible without the original project identity or internal implementation details.
Restricted-detail overview
Only the broad domain, role, and public boundary are disclosed.

Allowlist criteria

  • The public purpose is clear and professionally relevant.
  • The exact wording does not expose personal, client, partner, commercial, legal, security, credential, or confidential information.
  • The description does not reveal private architecture, internal methods, source locations, operating records, or unpublished performance data.
  • Academic work is separated from independent research ownership and industrial experience.
  • Business or client-adjacent work does not imply publication authority for the underlying project.
  • Private learning systems do not expose course names, providers, syllabi, labs, access details, or licensed materials.

Explicit claim boundaries

  • The B.Sc. is not described as completed while final requirements remain.
  • Academic silicon-photonics simulations are not presented as fabrication, tape-out, PDK, or industrial PIC experience.
  • Quantum-circuit sensitivity work is not presented as demonstrated quantum error correction.
  • Course laboratory work is not presented as independent research or industrial characterization.
  • AI and automation work is described through professional value rather than private architecture or operating methods.
  • Development-stage sensing work is not presented as validated performance or operational readiness.

Information intentionally excluded

  • Original names of private business initiatives, product concepts, client systems, and private learning programs.
  • Provider names, course names, syllabus structures, private labs, licensed materials, and source-owned course content.
  • Client and partner identities, contact details, financial terms, contracts, proposals, pricing, targets, and sales information.
  • Credentials, private endpoints, repository paths, source data, access configurations, source locations, and storage topology.
  • Internal project IDs, department structures, decision ledgers, schedules, operational runbooks, and proprietary methods.
  • Agreement-restricted material, security assumptions, topology, performance targets, and mission-specific capabilities.
  • Private academic submissions, advisor correspondence, restricted exam material, and unpublished results.
