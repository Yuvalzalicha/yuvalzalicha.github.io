# Project Disclosure Policy

The public portfolio represents major projects at a high level while protecting confidential, personal, client-owned, security-sensitive, academic, commercial, and proprietary information.

## Publishable

- Academic project names when public identification is appropriate
- Generalized titles for business, product, client-adjacent, and private learning-system work
- Broad purpose, domain, maturity, and candidate role
- Transferable skills stated without revealing internal methods
- Sanitized academic methods or results appropriate for public release
- Explicit claim boundaries

## Excluded

- Original names of business initiatives, product concepts, client systems, and private learning programs
- Provider names, course names, syllabus structures, private labs, licensed materials, and source-owned course content
- Client names, contact details, financial terms, contracts, proposals, pricing, and commercial targets
- Credentials, private endpoints, repository paths, internal source data, access configurations, and source locations
- Internal project IDs, department structures, decision ledgers, operational runbooks, and proprietary methods
- Agreement-restricted content, mission-specific capabilities, security assumptions, topology, and performance targets
- Unpublished research data, private academic submissions, advisor correspondence, and restricted exam material
- Claims of deployment, fabrication, independent research ownership, or traction not supported at the stated level

## Disclosure labels

- Detailed academic case study
- Generalized public overview
- Anonymized architecture overview
- Restricted-detail overview

## Public contact-data policy

The public site and public CV expose email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and country only. A telephone number is reserved for application copies sent directly to employers.
